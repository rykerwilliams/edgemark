How Edgemark works

Every deck gets a slot (a fixed position along the top edge of the inner sleeve) and a pen colour. A card's sleeve carries one mark for each deck that uses that copy, so you can fan a box and see which decks a card belongs to.

Pools

Decks in the same pool share copies because you never sleeve two of them at once, for example your Old School decks. Copy 1 carries the marks of every deck in the pool that runs the card, copy 2 the decks that run two or more, and so on. A pool therefore needs as many copies as its hungriest deck. Decks in different pools, or decks set to dedicated (signed decks, a cube, anything that stays built), get copies of their own.

Marking a card

  1. Choose your starting corner and finish your lists before you paint.
  2. Slide the Perfect Fit inner into the printed guide, card face up, top edge against the stop.
  3. Paint each slot listed for the card through its window: the stripe at the very top edge, any variant dot in the lower half of the window. The long windows are slots 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 and 24, and the notch marks slot 1. For side B (slots 25 to 48), flip the sleeve and count the same way.
  4. Let it dry fully (touch-dry isn't sleeve-ready), outer-sleeve it, then tick it off here.

Variants

When one deck has several builds (say an Atog list with a Fling package and one without), split it into variants instead of spending more slots. The deck keeps one slot. A card that every variant plays gets just the stripe at the top of the window. A card only some variants play also gets a dot in the lower half of the same window, in that variant's colour. When you build a variant, pull cards with the bare stripe plus that variant's dot.

Tokens and cubes

Tokens, emblems and other extras no decklist names go under Decks → Tokens and extras, with the decks that use them. A CubeCobra import turns each tag you tick into its own deck in one pool, so a card with two tags carries both marks and the cube needs just one copy of it.

Changing lists

Re-sync an Archidekt deck, edit a pasted list, or plan it in Swaps. Edgemark compares what's painted with what's needed and shows the smallest set of marks to add or clear. When a deck is deleted, its paint stays on the sleeves until you clear it, so those marks show up under Marks to clear.

Swaps applied here change only Edgemark's copy. Make the same edit on Archidekt, or the next sync will undo it.
